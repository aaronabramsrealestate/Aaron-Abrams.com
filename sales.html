What it means for you

With about 3.0 months of supply, the market sits between a buyer’s and a seller’s market. Well-priced, well-prepared homes still move, and buyers have more room to negotiate on condos and townhomes. A home value review or a buyer strategy call shows how these numbers apply to your neighborhood.
