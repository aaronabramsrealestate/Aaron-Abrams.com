North CountySan Diego Proper

North County

North County runs from the coast at Del Mar up to Oceanside and inland to the foothills. Most of these places are separate cities, each with its own city government and permit rules, and school districts often cross city lines. Rancho Santa Fe, Fallbrook and Bonsall are unincorporated communities governed by San Diego County.

North County Coast

Oceanside

Separate city

A historic pier, a revived downtown, and a wide range of homes from beach streets to hillside neighborhoods.

Carlsbad

Separate city

A walkable beach village, three coastal lagoons, and planned neighborhoods from the coast to the hills.

Encinitas

Separate city

Five communities, surf breaks, and Coast Highway 101 charm.

Solana Beach

Separate city

Cedros Design District, quiet coves, and a train station a short walk from the beach.

Del Mar

Separate city

A walkable beach village with bluff-top views and the famous fairgrounds.

North County Inland

Rancho Santa Fe

Unincorporated community

Acreage estates, riding trails, and a historic village core.

Poway

Separate city

Known as The City in the Country, with bigger lots, mountain views and easy I-15 access.

Escondido

Separate city

A large inland city with a historic downtown, the Safari Park nearby, and everything from bungalows to acreage.

San Marcos

Separate city

A university city with the San Elijo Hills community, a growing downtown, and Lake San Marcos next door.

Vista

Separate city

Craft breweries, downtown live theater, and hillside neighborhoods a short drive from the coast.

Fallbrook

Unincorporated community

Avocado groves, horse properties and a village center, in a rural community off I-15.

Bonsall

Unincorporated community

A rural, equestrian community along the San Luis Rey River with groves and larger parcels.

San Diego Proper

These are neighborhoods inside the City of San Diego itself, so they share one city government and one set of city permit rules. Most are served by San Diego Unified, though parts of the north city are served by other districts. Del Mar, Solana Beach, Poway and the other cities in the North County list above are separate cities, not San Diego neighborhoods.

Central San Diego

Downtown San Diego

City of San Diego neighborhood

High-rise condos, the waterfront, Petco Park, and the trolley and train hub of the county.

Little Italy

City of San Diego neighborhood

A walkable waterfront district of restaurants, the Saturday Mercato, and modern condos.

Hillcrest

City of San Diego neighborhood

Craftsman homes, a hospital district, and a lively walkable core beside Balboa Park.

North Park

City of San Diego neighborhood

Craftsman bungalows, a busy arts and dining scene, and a walkable heart on University Avenue.

South Park

City of San Diego neighborhood

A small-scale neighborhood of bungalows, courtyards and local shops along Fern and 30th Streets.

University Heights

City of San Diego neighborhood

Craftsman homes, canyon edges, and a village feel along Park Boulevard and Adams Avenue.

Normal Heights

City of San Diego neighborhood

Bungalows, canyon edges, and Adams Avenue antique shops, music and street fairs.

Kensington

City of San Diego neighborhood

Spanish and Tudor style homes on curving streets, with a small-town feel above Mission Valley.

Mission Valley

North City and Inland

Carmel Valley

City of San Diego neighborhood

University City

Next to UC San Diego, with trolley access, Westfield UTC and canyon open space.

How to choose the right community

Start with what your days actually look like. Think about your commute, how close you want to be to the beach, how much yard you need, and whether walkability or space matters more. Then compare total monthly cost, including HOA dues and any Mello-Roos tax, not just list price.

  • Near the sand and walkable: Del Mar, Solana Beach, the beach communities of Encinitas, Carlsbad Village, Oceanside, Pacific Beach, Ocean Beach and La Jolla.
  • Urban and walkable: Downtown, Little Italy, Hillcrest, North Park and South Park.
  • Parks, trails, and planned communities: Carmel Valley, San Elijo Hills in San Marcos, Rancho Bernardo and Scripps Ranch.
  • Land, privacy, and horses: Rancho Santa Fe, Poway, Fallbrook and Bonsall.
  • Older homes with character: University Heights, Kensington, Normal Heights and Point Loma.
