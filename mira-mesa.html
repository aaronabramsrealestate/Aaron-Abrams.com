Mira Mesa homes and neighborhood guide

A large inland neighborhood of 1970s and 1980s tract homes and townhomes, with canyon preserves and some of the county's major employers nearby.

Living in Mira Mesa

Mira Mesa grew quickly in the 1970s and 1980s, and its layout of neighborhoods, parks and shopping centers along Mira Mesa Boulevard reflects that growth. The neighborhood is close to employment centers in Sorrento Mesa and Kearny Mesa, and it has a wide range of restaurants and bakeries along Mira Mesa Boulevard.

Los Peñasquitos Canyon Preserve borders the north side with long trails, and Mira Mesa Community Park and several neighborhood parks give residents room to play. The community sits next to Marine Corps Air Station Miramar, which shapes parts of the landscape.

Homes you'll find

  • Single family tract homes: from the 1970s and 1980s, in many sizes.
  • Townhomes and condos: across the neighborhood, often with HOA dues.
  • Apartments and rentals: a large part of the housing.
  • Homes near canyon open space: with views and trail access.
  • Homes with additions or ADUs: where owners have expanded.

Getting around and schools

Mira Mesa is served by San Diego Unified School District, with Mira Mesa High among its schools, and the district also offers choice and magnet programs. Assignments depend on the exact address, so confirm directly with the district.

I-15, SR-56, I-805 and SR-163 give quick access north and south, and Mira Mesa Boulevard is the main east to west route. Rapid and local buses serve the main streets.

Buying in Mira Mesa

Marine Corps Air Station Miramar sits nearby, and jet noise varies a lot depending on where the property is relative to the flight paths. Visit at different times of day and ask neighbors. Homes from the 1970s and 1980s deserve careful inspection of roofs, plumbing and electrical systems.

For townhomes and condos, review the HOA budget, reserves and rules on rentals. Ask about special assessments and the history of major repairs.

Selling in Mira Mesa

Mira Mesa buyers often compare many similar homes, so highlight what makes yours different: updates, the lot, the floor plan, the view or the parking. Clear information about condition and permits helps.

Show proximity to trails, parks and the freeway and be upfront about aircraft noise where it applies. Buyers appreciate honesty.

Mira Mesa FAQ

Where is Mira Mesa?

In the north central part of San Diego, between I-15 and I-805, south of Rancho Peñasquitos and near Marine Corps Air Station Miramar.

Is aircraft noise a concern in Mira Mesa?

It can be, depending on the property and its position relative to the flight paths at Marine Corps Air Station Miramar. Visit at different times of day.

What types of homes are in Mira Mesa?

Single family tract homes, townhomes, condos and apartments, mostly from the 1970s and 1980s.

What is there to do in Mira Mesa?

Parks, the Los Peñasquitos Canyon Preserve trails and a wide range of restaurants and bakeries along Mira Mesa Boulevard.
